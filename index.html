About me

I work across two disciplines that meet more often than people expect. As an ICT professional I teach and manage technology; as a graphic designer I turn an institution’s information, such as programmes, fees, entry requirements and dates, into material people can read at a glance.

Much of the work on this page was made for DMI–St. John the Baptist University across its Mangochi and Blantyre campuses; the rest is logos, business cards, adverts, invitations and album art for businesses, community groups, churches and families. That means designing for many formats at once: an A4 flyer, a brochure spread, a square post for Facebook and a 7-metre gate sign all have to feel like the same university.

Outside work I run free digital skills sessions for secondary school students in Mangochi District, including Mangochi Secondary, Nansenga CDSS and St Monica Girls.
